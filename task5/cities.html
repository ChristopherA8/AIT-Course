Zagreb

Zagreb is the capital and largest city of Croatia, located in the northwest of the country along the Sava River and at the southern slopes of the Medvednica Mountain. As the nation's political, economic, and cultural heart, it is home to hundreds of thousands of residents and serves as a major transportation hub. The city features a distinct dual layout, consisting of the historic Upper Town with its medieval cobblestone streets, Gothic churches, and government buildings, alongside the bustling Lower Town known for its nineteenth-century architecture, open squares, and lively pedestrian zones. Zagreb offers a vibrant cafe culture, numerous museums, theaters, and expansive green spaces like Maksimir Park, blending Central European charm with a dynamic modern atmosphere.
